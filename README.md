## Introduction

RetailMartt is an e-commerce company operating in the retail industry across multiple locations in Nigeria. The company sells everyday consumer products online and relies heavily on smooth order processing and reliable payments to drive revenue.
This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior, sales performance, and order outcomes using SQL. The goal is to understand what drives revenue, where orders fail, and how operational issues such as payment failures impact completed sales.

## Problem Statement
RetailMartt is experiencing high order cancellations and failed payments, which may be limiting revenue growth. While sales appear healthy, management lacks clarity on:
Which products and categories drive the most revenue
Why many orders are not completed
Which customers and locations contribute most to sales
This analysis aims to provide clarity on revenue drivers, customer value, and operational bottlenecks to support better business decisions.
